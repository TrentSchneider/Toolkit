Preset Behavior Explained

Presets do NOT change breakpoints or structure. They only adjust the intensity of scaling across the system.

Compact

  • Reduces spacing growth (0.85×)
  • Reduces typography growth (0.9×)
  • Flatter, denser scaling curve

Balanced (Default)

  • No scaling modification (1×)
  • Pure interpolation between breakpoints

Wide

  • Increases spacing growth (1.2×)
  • Increases typography growth (1.25×)
  • More aggressive responsive scaling

⚠️ Presets affect slope magnitude only — not the math model itself.
